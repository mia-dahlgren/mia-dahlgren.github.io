SLODOWN was founded by three Cal Poly alumni trying to contribute to the community. Born out of a shared appreciation for connection and craft, the shop was envisioned as a welcoming neighborhood fixture where people could pause, catch their breath, and connect with one another right here in San Luis Obispo.

The name was inspired by the meditative nature of making the tea, and it influenced how we built our business. From the deliberate sourcing of our ingredients to the peaceful ambiance of our shop, every detail is crafted to encourage mindfulness and provide a peaceful sanctuary away from the rush of everyday life.

We wanted to build a comforting, well thought-out space easily accessible by public transit. Whether you are stopping by between classes, meeting a friend, or looking for a quiet corner to unwind, we love matcha and can't wait to share our passion with you.
